- PLP-326: every escalation receipt written from now on carries `decided_at`, the UTC time observed when the escalated decision was recorded (`escalation.py`); `routing kpi-export` uses it as the observed time of the `escalation` record and keeps the deduced upper bound, marked `deduced`, for older receipts. No routing or escalation decision reads it (`docs/kpi-export.md`). Foundry 2.6.0 and earlier reject a ledger holding such a receipt as invalid, so routing fails closed for that issue there: do not go back to such a version, or run one on another host, once a new escalation receipt exists.
